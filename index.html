How Cuberence helps

Your client already gives you the opportunity. Cuberence helps you find it.

A destination, a travel window, a preferred trip length and a few constraints are enough. Cuberence navigates those constraints and looks for mini-destinations that can fit naturally into the journey.

Family visit · Turkey
Traveller

“I’m travelling to Turkey to see my family. I can leave October 10–15 and stay around 10–12 days. What would the ticket cost?”

Advisor + Cuberence

“The standard trip is around $1,300. But for approximately $250 more, you could spend 2 nights in Paris along the way.”

Same trip purpose.A richer itinerary and another sales opportunity.
Flexible discovery · Germany
Traveller

“I’m going to Germany next month, but I’d love to explore somewhere else too.”

Advisor + Cuberence

“There are viable options through London, Paris, Milan and Rome. Which interests you most?”

Traveller

“Milan and London both sound interesting.”

Advisor + Cuberence

“If you travel on the 10th, you could spend 2 nights in Milan with essentially the same flight cost as the standard itinerary. Would you like to discuss it further?”

Hotel, transfers, activities and other destination costs remain separate.

Long journey · Canada → Bangkok
Traveller

“I’m planning to travel to Bangkok between October 20 and 25.”

Advisor + Cuberence

“That’s a long journey. Would you be open to spending a night or two somewhere along the way?”

Traveller

“Sure.”

Advisor + Cuberence

“If you travel on the 21st, you could consider Dubai, Amman (Jordan), or Istanbul. Istanbul looks especially promising for a 3-night stay. Would you like me to explore that option further?”

Why this is difficult to do manually

The opportunity is easy to imagine. Finding it is the hard part.

Cuberence screens hundreds of date, route and stay combinations in seconds to uncover mini-destinations an advisor is unlikely to find by searching one itinerary at a time.
