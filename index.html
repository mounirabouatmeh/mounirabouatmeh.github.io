03 / ~30 seconds with Cuberence

Cuberence does the complex work for you.

Adding a mini-destination to an existing trip takes more than choosing a city. The flight itinerary must be reworked around your client’s dates, flexibility and travel constraints to make the stayover possible while protecting their onward journey.

That can mean evaluating 1,000+ possible flight and stay combinations.

Cuberence brings together flexible dates, connecting cities, airlines and 1–3-night stays — identifying the most promising options and highlighting potential travel risks.
