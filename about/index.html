About us

It started with a travel opportunity we kept finding — but only through a lot of manual work.

As travelers and travel professionals, we saw a gap between the trip a client asks for and the richer journey that may already be hidden inside it.

The gap we saw

We kept discovering more inside ordinary trips.

A traveler would come with a destination, a range of possible dates and a few constraints. Yet hidden inside that same trip were often other possibilities — a city that could be added along the way, a different departure date that opened a better itinerary, or a connection that could become another experience.

We found these opportunities surprisingly often. The problem was finding them.

What we did

So we started doing the work ourselves.

For our own trips and for travelers we helped, we explored different dates, routes, stopovers and stay lengths manually.

And it worked. We found itineraries that allowed travelers to experience an additional destination — sometimes for a relatively small difference in airfare and occasionally for nearly the same flight cost.

But finding them meant testing combination after combination. It took time. A lot of it.

If it takes this much work for us to uncover these opportunities, how many are travel advisors missing every day simply because there isn’t enough time to search for them?
The idea

We decided to make the approach available to every travel agency.

Instead of keeping this way of searching for ourselves, we decided to turn the process into technology that travel advisors could use in their everyday work.

Cuberence was born from that idea: take the extensive manual exploration we were already doing and make it possible to screen dates, routes and stay combinations in seconds.

Our goal is not to replace the travel advisor. It is to give the advisor a capability that would otherwise be extremely difficult to perform manually at scale.

Where we are going

From something we discovered to something agencies everywhere can use.

What started as a way for us to find better travel possibilities became a bigger question:

What if every travel advisor could look at an ordinary client inquiry and discover opportunities that neither the advisor nor the traveler originally knew were there?

That is what we are building — together with travel agencies and industry partners who understand that a richer trip can also become a better business opportunity.

Our mission

Help travel agencies find the hidden trip inside every trip.

Give travel advisors technology that expands what they can discover for their clients, while keeping the advisor’s expertise, judgement and client relationship at the centre of the experience.
