About us

It started with a travel opportunity we kept finding — but only through a lot of manual work.

As travelers and travel professionals, we saw a gap between the trip a client asks for and the richer journey that may already be hidden inside it.

The gap we saw

We kept discovering more inside ordinary trips.

A traveler would come with a destination, a range of possible dates and a few constraints. Yet hidden inside that same trip were often other possibilities — a city that could be added along the way, a different departure date that opened a better itinerary, or a connection that could become another experience.

We found these opportunities surprisingly often. The problem was finding them.

What we did

So we started doing the work ourselves.

For our own trips and for travelers we helped, we explored different dates, routes, stopovers and stay lengths manually.

And it worked. We found itineraries that allowed travelers to experience an additional destination — sometimes for a relatively small difference in airfare and occasionally for nearly the same flight cost.

But finding them meant testing combination after combination. It took time. A lot of it.

How many opportunities could you uncover for your clients if you didn’t have to search every combination manually?
The idea

We built Cuberence for travel advisors and agency owners.

Rather than keep this approach to ourselves, we began building a tool you can use when planning your clients’ trips.

Cuberence screens dates, routes and short stays in seconds so you can focus on the opportunities worth presenting.

Your expertise remains essential. Cuberence helps you uncover the options; you decide what to recommend and book.

Where we are going

A better way to serve your clients — and grow your business.

What started as a way for us to find better travel possibilities became a bigger question:

What if your next routine client request held an experience neither you nor your client had thought to explore?

That is what we are building with travel advisors, agency owners and industry partners: more possibilities for your clients and more sales opportunities for your agency.

Our mission

Help you find the hidden trip inside every trip.

Give you more ways to uncover valuable experiences for your clients — while keeping your judgement, your suppliers and your client relationships at the centre of every offer.
